# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Dominyx Network a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience, or status.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Keep discussions technical and welcome newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized content, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through the security contact listed in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
